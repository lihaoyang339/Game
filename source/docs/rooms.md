# 环境美术设计说明 · Ground floor vertical slice

Plan: `docs/floorplan-ground.png`. It is generated from the same room bounds the game builds (`tools/floorplan.mjs`), so the drawing can't drift from the build.
Each room's code lives in `src/rooms/*.js`, and every room's header comment states its design intent.

## 总体原则
- **Layout:** No central hall radiating outward. The foyer only opens to the gallery (an arch) and the living room (double doors). The gallery is the spine that links the study and dining room. The service wing returns to the west end of the gallery through a jib door, which can only be opened from the service side (the shortcut). There is a 1.3 m void between the dining room and the study that appears on no drawing; this sets up the spatial puzzle.
- **Era:** A country house, roughly 1880–1900, built in stages. The front rooms are formal. The service wing is utilitarian. The conservatory is a later cast-iron-and-glass extension.
- **Ageing has a cause:** Dust only settles on upward-facing surfaces. Dirt rises from the floor. Water stains run down from leak points (the corner of the foyer ceiling, the living-room window). Fading is strongest where light reaches. A painting that has been taken down leaves a clean, unfaded rectangle and its nail. We do not lay one uniform layer of grime over everything.
- **Lighting is always motivated:** Window light comes from the storm (cold blue, with lightning that lights the interior). Warm pools come from the fireplace, oil lamps, wall sconces and bare bulbs. Spill light comes from neighbouring rooms. No room is evenly lit. The middle of the gallery is deliberately left dark.
- **Construction:** All mouldings are extruded from real profiles: baseboard, chair rail, cornice, picture rail and architraves. Wainscoting has stiles, rails and raised panels with bevels. Doors have six panels and brass hardware. Windows are sash windows with glazing bars. Furniture comes from the CC0 photoscan library, and the pieces it doesn't cover (desk, dining table, bookcases, range, stone sink) are modelled from turned and moulded profiles.

## Fixed cameras
18 cameras, two to three per room. Switching is driven by zones (`ctx.cam` in each room). The rules:
- Every camera is placed at a corner or the end of a long axis and framed against the room's focal point. Doors and windows are used as frames within the frame.
- Track is 0.1–0.18: the camera only drifts slightly with the player. The shot stays fixed, but the player remains readable.
- Movement is relative to the camera. While a key is held, the original direction is kept across camera cuts, so the player doesn't suddenly reverse.

## Known limitations of the art (honest list)
- Characters use Quaternius' CC0 "Superhero" base body, so the proportions are broad in the shoulders and narrow at the waist. Clothing is painted on in the shader with geometry smoothing; it is not a real cloth mesh.
- Glass uses transparent plus a rain shader, not real refraction. The storm outside the windows is a painted backdrop, not 3D terrain.
- There is no real-time GI. Bounce light is approximated with a low-intensity environment map and a hemisphere light.
- The cloud QA environment has no GPU (SwiftShader software rendering), so the QA images are rendered at 960×540. On a real GPU the game runs at full resolution.
